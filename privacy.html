Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

PedalStock collects as little as possible. The iPhone and iPad app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The web app works the same way unless you choose to sign in, which saves your inventory and builds to an account, as described below. This policy explains what PedalStock stores and the few times it uses the network.

What stays on your device

Your parts inventory, builds, progress, notes and attached files (schematics, layouts, build docs and photos) are stored only on your iPhone or iPad, inside PedalStock. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

The web version

The PedalStock web app at pedalstockapp.com/app keeps your inventory, builds and the files you attach to them in your web browser's own storage on your computer. Unless you sign in, nothing is sent to us or stored on a server, and the site uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. Clearing your browser's data for the site deletes it, so export your inventory and builds as a backup; the builds export includes attached files.

Scanning a schematic, layout or PDF in the web app happens in your browser too: the file is read on your computer and never uploaded. The first time you scan, your browser downloads the text-recognition engine from this site.

Web app accounts (optional)

Signing in to the web app is optional. It lets you use your inventory and builds in any browser. If you sign in:

  • What's stored: your email address (or the name and email Google or Apple shares when you sign in with them), an account ID, and when the account was created and last signed in to; and your inventory and builds, including parts, quantities, notes and build progress. Attached files are not uploaded; they stay in your browser.
  • Where: with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which runs the account system and database on PedalStock's behalf. Google's handling of this data is described in Firebase's privacy information.
  • Who can see it: only you. The database only lets a signed-in account read and change its own data. As the operator, PedalStock's developer can reach the database through Firebase's console, and only does so to fix a problem you report.
  • What it's used for: signing you in and keeping your data in sync. It is never sold, shared, used for advertising or analysed.
  • Emails: sign-in links and password resets are sent by Firebase from a PedalStock address. You won't get any other email.
  • In your browser: signing in keeps a sign-in token and a copy of your data in the browser's storage, so the app works offline. Signing out removes both.
  • Deleting it: in the web app, open Account → Delete Account to delete your account and everything saved in it straight away. Or email info@crankynerdcustoms.com and it will be deleted for you.

When the app uses the network

PedalStock works offline. It connects to the internet only when you ask it to:

  • Find Schematic or BOM Online sends the words you search for to the public search feeds of Tagboard Effects (Blogger), PedalPCB, Aion FX, madbeanpedals, PedalLayouts and ElectroSmash. Those sites receive your search terms and, like any website, your IP address. PedalStock sends nothing else.
  • Opening a search result shows that website inside the app, and downloading a file from it fetches that file. The website's own privacy policy applies to what you view there.
  • Links on the Resources tab and "Search the Whole Web in Safari" open in Safari.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose Scan with Camera to scan a schematic or layout. Photos are read only when you pick them with Choose from Photos. Scans and photos are stored with the build on your device. Text recognition in scans runs on the device using Apple's frameworks.

Children

PedalStock does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
