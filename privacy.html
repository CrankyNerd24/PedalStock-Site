Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

PedalStock collects as little as possible. It's a free web app with no analytics, no advertising, no cookies and no tracking. Unless you choose to sign in, everything stays in your browser. This policy explains what PedalStock stores and when it uses the network.

What stays in your browser

The PedalStock web app at pedalstockapp.com/app keeps your inventory, builds, progress, notes and the files you attach to builds in your web browser's own storage on your computer or phone. Unless you sign in, none of it is sent to us or stored on a server. Clearing your browser's data for the site deletes it, so export your inventory and builds as a backup; the builds export includes attached files.

Your colour theme and a few display choices, such as the Build Shortages filter, are also remembered in your browser. They never leave it.

Scanning

Scanning a schematic, layout or PDF happens in your browser: the file is read on your computer or phone and never uploaded. The first time you scan, your browser downloads the text-recognition engine from this site.

Accounts (optional)

Signing in is optional. It lets you use your inventory and builds in any browser. If you sign in:

  • What's stored: your email address (or the name and email Google or Apple shares when you sign in with them), an account ID, and when the account was created and last signed in to; and your inventory and builds, including parts, quantities, notes and build progress. Attached files are not uploaded; they stay in your browser.
  • Where: with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which runs the account system and database on PedalStock's behalf. Google's handling of this data is described in Firebase's privacy information.
  • Who can see it: only you. The database only lets a signed-in account read and change its own data. As the operator, PedalStock's developer can reach the database through Firebase's console, and only does so to fix a problem you report.
  • What it's used for: signing you in and keeping your data in sync. It is never sold, shared, used for advertising or analysed.
  • Emails: sign-in links and password resets are sent by Firebase from a PedalStock address. You won't get any other email.
  • In your browser: signing in keeps a sign-in token and a copy of your data in the browser's storage, so you can keep working if your connection drops. Signing out removes both.
  • Deleting it: in the web app, open Account → Delete Account to delete your account and everything saved in it straight away. Or email info@crankynerdcustoms.com and it will be deleted for you.

Tips

The Tip Jar link opens PedalStock's page on Ko-fi. Nothing from Ko-fi is loaded on this site or in the web app until you click it. If you leave a tip, Ko-fi and its payment provider handle the payment, and their privacy policies apply; PedalStock only receives what Ko-fi passes on with the tip, such as your name and any message.

Children

PedalStock isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and it will be deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
