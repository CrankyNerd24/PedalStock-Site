Support

New to PedalStock? The user guide walks through everything, step by step.

Have a question, found a bug, or want a feature? Email info@crankynerdcustoms.com with your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version, and a screenshot if something looks wrong.

How do I import my existing inventory?

Export your spreadsheet as CSV (Numbers, Excel and Google Sheets all can), then in PedalStock go to Inventory → + → Import Inventory…. Only name and category columns are required. Other columns the app reads: quantity, value, capacitance, capacitorType, voltageRating, lowStockThreshold, supplier, price, purchaseLink, commonlyUsed and notes.

How do I add a new order on top of what I have?

Use an addQuantity column instead of quantity. Parts you already have get that many added; new ones are created. Parts are matched by name, or by category and value, so 10k finds your "10kΩ Resistor".

How do I turn a parts list into a build?

On the Builds tab, tap + and choose Paste BOM…, Import Project… or Scan Schematic/Layout…. For a build you've already started, open it and use Import Parts From PDF or Image…. You'll see what was read before anything is imported.

Why does a part show as short when the build looks covered?

Build Shortages adds up every Planning and In Progress build at once. If two builds each need five of a part and you have eight, both rows look fine on their own but you're two short overall. Use the Build menu at the top of Build Shortages to see one pedal at a time.

Is my data backed up?

Your inventory and builds are stored on your device and included in your iPhone or iPad backup. They don't sync between devices.

Can I use the web app in more than one browser?

Yes. In the web app, choose Sign In (at the right of the Inventory, Builds and Low Stock tabs) and sign in with an email link, a password, Google or Apple. Your inventory and builds then save to your account and appear in any browser you sign in on. Attached files stay in each browser for now. The iPhone app doesn't use web accounts yet, so move data between it and the web with export and import.

How do I move my inventory and builds to another device?

Go to Inventory → + → Export Inventory… and save the file, for example to iCloud Drive, or save it and then AirDrop or email it. On the other device, use Import Inventory… and pick that file. Then move your builds the same way: Builds → + → Export Builds…, and Import Builds… on the other device. Import the inventory first, so each build's parts match up.
