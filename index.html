An inventory that speaks capacitor.

Every part sorted into collapsible categories, with stock you can read at a glance.

  • Capacitors show their value in pF, nF and µF, sorted by value and then voltage.
  • "1K ohm", "1k" and "1k0" are the same resistor, so imports and duplicate checks match them.
  • Import a whole spreadsheet as CSV, or add stock from a new order on top of what you have.

Exactly what to order, build by build.

Build Shortages adds up what every open build needs against what's in your drawers, or narrows it to the one pedal you're shopping for.

  • Share the list straight to Notes or a message to your supplier.
  • Swap in what you have: a TL062 for a TL072, or a greenie for a box film, and the build remembers the substitution.
  • Interchangeable capacitor types are suggested for you; electrolytics and tantalums are never mixed in.

Track the build, bench step by bench step.

Tick parts off as you solder them and work through the stages from drilling the enclosure to the first test.

  • Ticking a part takes it out of inventory, so your stock is right even halfway through a build.
  • Mark the build complete and the rest comes out; reopen it and you choose whether parts go back.
  • Keep the schematic, layout and build doc with the build, as PDFs, photos or camera scans.
